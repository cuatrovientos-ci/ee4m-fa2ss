# logistics applied to the supply chain

**DESCRIPTION** The module provides an in-depth exploration of logistics, focusing on both supply and warehouse logistics. It covers demand management, including the influence of Industry 4.0 and emerging trends, as well as collaborative forecasting and planning strategies, such as PUSH, PULL and hybrid approaches. In addition, the module addresses the organization of transport processes, sustainable transport development, agreements with service providers and the strategic design of distribution structures, equipping participants with the necessary skills to optimize logistics operations effectively.

**COMPETENCIES**

- Analytical thinking skills
- Strategic competencies
- Sustainable strategic planning and technical competencies
- Problem solving skills

It begins with an introduction to supply logistics, followed by an in-depth examination of demand management processes. This includes an overview of new trends in supply chains, with a focus on Industry 4.0 and its impact on demand management.
